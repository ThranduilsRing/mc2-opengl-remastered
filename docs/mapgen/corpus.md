# Authored MC2 layout corpus

Generated by `py -3 -B tools/mapgen/analyze.py`.

Extracted **320** mission/FIT pairs from seven installs; **0** extraction errors. Stock calibration uses exactly mc2_01..mc2_24.

## Method and limits

Distances are movement cells (multiply by 42.6667 for world units). Tables show median [p10..p90], not design mandates. Structural clusters use same-team single-link distance <=12 cells (512 wu), retaining >=3 objects or any turret. Walls within 8 cells of a structure join its bounds; >=6 wall pieces defines a walled compound. This can merge adjacent sites and split sparse bases. Archetype labels are explicit heuristics: walls first, then turret batteries, airfield/HQ names, industrial mix >=35%, town mix >=40%, otherwise outpost. Building roles are name-based and inspectable in common.py. Towns can be neutral. Props, trees, rocks and fences do not become structural clusters.

Feature distances use an eight-neighbour (Chebyshev) transform on the original 3-cell vertex grid. Objects are point centres: no inferred model footprints. Slope uses finite differences, steep >=35 deg, flat <10 deg. Water uses bit 0. Paved areas use terrain types 10,13..20; roads use decoded overlays except rough. Vertex footprints and road junction counts are approximate. No claim of full MOVE passability, scripted objectives or vehicle traversal is made.

All paired maps were extracted, including tutorial, multiplayer and local test maps. Comparisons below use custom mod stems absent from the stock install; mods without custom stems use their 24 inherited campaign maps. Thus inherited recataloging does not masquerade as new mission design. The pooled generator profile uses stock campaign + custom maps, deduplicated by decoded terrain/object packet SHA-256. Mod-specific tables may still share maps. Source provenance is in the local index and a compact committed extraction_manifest.json; full mission JSON is regenerable, ignored local data.

## Generator calibration

`tools/mapgen/profile.json` stores measured distributions, role mixes and link ranges, with the extraction-index SHA-256. The generator samples from these ranges, constraining extreme chained clusters to usable parcel dimensions. It prefers open sites in the measured proportions, puts roads first, and strengthens traversability/parent-chain invariants beyond what coarse authored data can prove. Synthetic terrain and rectangular parcels are implementation choices, not measured stock rules.
