## Engine triggers and interpretation

The following original trigger inventory remains useful; the current binding/import analysis above supersedes its old structural-failure rule.

- `mclib/bdactor_type.cpp:330` reads `[Animation:N]`: AnimationName, LoopAnimation, Reverse, Random, StartFrame; `:359` resolves ASE/AGL. `mclib/bdactor.cpp:587` selects gestures; `:643` starts reverse at the last frame; `:656` applies loop and frame rate. `mclib/bdactor_update.cpp:51` advances frames. ## Coverage, reproducibility and limits

Run `py -3 tools/anim_parity/audit.py` from this checkout. Outputs: REPORT.md, inventory.json and nine gate pose sheets, under the external proof directory. `inventory.json` binds each effective stock input and each remake LOD to SHA256 and records the exact selected bake path, every candidate path, node parents/pivots/triangle counts, transformed extrema, and 21 gate pose bounds. Loose overrides take precedence. Revisions are selected by newest LOD0 file modification time per asset, with deterministic path ordering on ties; reruns can change when the art lane rebakes. All source installs and art lanes remain read-only.

The inventory scans stock FST appearance declarations plus effective loose INIs. Vehicle/mech declarations and uncatalogued unused templates are listed separately in `excluded_declarations`; missing declared meshes are explicitly unresolved. All exported pristine remakes without a stock animation table/aim node/SpinMe are listed in `remakes_without_stock_animation`. Their static decorative doors are not invented engine animations: notably milcheckpoint has gate_a/gate_b authoring intent, but the effective stock milcheckpoint.ini has no animation entries and the FST has no matching AGL clip. There is no stock-triggered barrier motion to preserve there.

The revised tool binds by case-insensitive name and distinguishes REAL failures from warnings. Missing/empty moving parts, lost animated ancestors in the current cooker, and position-track frame errors above 1 WU are REAL. Static parent spelling, rotation-only pivot displacement above 0.05 WU, and stock-bounds growth are WARN. A pivot warning is not a claim of incorrect rotation about the authored part's own axis. Offline extreme poses use the flattened cooked mesh hierarchy rather than pretending the source glTF hierarchy survives cooking. Triangle/AABB gate clearance remains conservative; stock baseline flags expose that limitation. Candidate leaf measurements do not relabel or modify art.
