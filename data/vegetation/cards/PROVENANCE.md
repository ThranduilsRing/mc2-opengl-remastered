# Woody card provenance

`bush_round.png`, `bush_heath.png`, `sapling_leaf.png`, `sapling_pine.png` are
original procedural art generated by `procedural_woody_cards` in
`tools/cook/veg_card_cook.py`, seed 20261108 plus species index. Branches and
leaf ellipses are drawn mathematically; no third-party images, models, fonts
or training output are used in the shipped cards. Project-owned source art.
The contact sheet labels use Pillow's preview font and are not shipped art.

All cards are 512x512 RGBA. The existing cook produces BC7 with ten mips,
coverage preservation, colour dilation and conservative eight-vertex strips.
The renderer supplies local biome/season colour and deterministic tint variation.
Two shrub and two sapling silhouettes share the eight-layer grass array.

## mc2_01 palette repaint (lane veg-palette-mc2_01, 2026-11-10)

All eight shipped cards were repainted by this lane with
`py -3 tools/cook/veg_card_cook.py --paint data/vegetation/palettes/mc2_01.json --output data/vegetation/cooked`:

- `grass_01..04.png`: new original blade fields drawn by `procedural_grass_cards`
  (seed 20261110 plus card index; tapering polyline blades drawn at 1024 px and
  downsampled to 512). Tones and per-card mixes (`grass_tones`, `grass_cards` in the
  palette) follow the owner's 2026-11-10 grass-field reference image (used only as a
  colour/feel reference, nothing copied from it), pulled toward mc2_01's khaki palette:
  straw dominant, olive roots, rust blades, seed heads, light backlit tips.
- `bush_round.png`, `bush_heath.png`, `sapling_leaf.png`, `sapling_pine.png`:
  regenerated by `procedural_woody_cards` (same seeds and silhouettes; the conifer
  tiers are now ragged) with leaf colours drawn from per-species palette ramps.

The ramps come from `data/vegetation/palettes/mc2_01.json` (k-means of mc2_01 day
captures plus stock palm/treeline foliage samples; provenance inside that file).
No third-party images. Cooked KTX2 files and `cards.json` were regenerated by the cook.
