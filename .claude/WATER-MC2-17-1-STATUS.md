# WATER-MC2-17-1 — mc2_17 shoreline "atrocious / smoothing line looks terrible"

Branch `claude/nifty-mendeleev`, HEAD at capture time `e1106885`.
Deploy lane `mc2-win64-v0.4`, manifest `src_commit e11068858d80` (= HEAD, clean tree).

## Verdict

`MC2_WATER_SHORE_MEANDER=75` (the AUDIT-TRUTH-1 default, `cdd91ad7`) is
**catastrophically over-scaled on mc2_17**. It is not producing a slightly-wrong
coastline; it is **carving away large regions of the water body** and revealing
the terrain underneath, with a blobby fBm-shaped pseudo-coastline. That is what
the user is looking at.

## Method

2x2 runtime gate matrix at pose `water_near`
(`tests/visual/bookmarks/mc2_17_terrain_near.json`, a pre-existing authored
shoreline pose), then an amplitude sweep. No source edits were needed for any
of it — both gates are runtime env vars.

**Noise floor: MEASURED, not recorded.** Arm A run twice, `--verify`:
`water_near` `9bcff26bd424` == `9bcff26bd424`, `land_near` `254a1daa20f7` ==
`254a1daa20f7`. Byte-identical => 0.000%. All PNGs verified non-zero
(24,887,323 bytes each; disk was at 12 GB).

## Result 1 — the shore MASK is completely inert on mc2_17 (my own hypothesis, REFUTED)

| `water_near` sha | mask=ON | mask=OFF |
|---|---|---|
| meander=75 | `9bcff26b…` | `9bcff26b…` |
| meander=0  | `d7dbf755…` | `d7dbf755…` |

A == C and B == D. Toggling `MC2_WATER_SHORE_MASK` changes **nothing**.
Confirmed a third way: `MC2_TERRAIN_SHORELINE=0`, which disables the mask
*load* outright, also yields `9bcff26b…` — byte-identical to default.

So my proposed mechanism — that `min(shore, shoreM)` hard-clips the meander
landward (razor edge) while leaving it free seaward (smudge) — is **dead**.
It was good algebra and wrong. The mask never participates on this map.

Two earlier hypotheses of mine were also refuted before this:
- *mask is low-res, hence the blur* — no: 3816 px over 15360 wu = **4.03 wu/texel**, far finer than the 18 wu smoothstep band.
- *the diagonal is the mask's alpha-coverage boundary* — no: decoding the PNG gives alpha>127 on **14,561,856 / 14,561,856 texels = 100.0%**, and `u_shoreMaskBounds` covers the whole map, so the `sl.a>0.5` and UV guards never fail.

**The brief's original prime suspect was right and I was wrong to redirect off it.**

## Result 2 — the meander is the sole driver, and the dose-response is linear

Pixels differing >8/255 from meander=0, at `water_near`:

| amplitude | frame changed |
|---|---|
| 15 | 8.87 % |
| 25 | 15.65 % |
| 45 | 27.60 % |
| **75 (default)** | **46.97 %** |

At the shipping default nearly **half the frame** changes. An edge treatment
cannot do that. Eyeballing A (75) vs B (0) side by side: at meander=0 the
region is an unbroken water body; at meander=75 a large dark landmass occupies
the upper-left with a soft, smeared, blobby boundary, plus a noticeably
straighter diagonal cut upper-right.

Mechanism: the meander perturbs `shoreWT` by `u_shoreMeanderWu * mn` where `mn`
carries a combined fBm gain of `1.60 + 0.80 = 2.40`, so amplitude 75 is a
**±180 wu** excursion. `shore = smoothstep(-shoreBlend*0.5, shoreBlend, shoreWT)`
with `shoreBlend = max(alphaDepth,1)` is a far narrower band than that, so over
mc2_17's flat shallow shelf the perturbation drives `shore` to 0 across wide
areas and the `if (shore <= 0.0) discard;` removes the water entirely.

This is exactly the documented residual of the gate — it is self-scaling by
terrain gradient (`displacement = A/|grad|`), so it is largest on flat terraces.
mc2_17 has a broad flat shelf. mc2_17 was never graded.

## Result 3 — the straight diagonal

**Not** a chunk/LOD seam and **not** a playable-area bound: it moves with the
meander amplitude and vanishes at meander=0, so it is generated by the discard
contour itself. It is the fBm zero-crossing running steeply across the flat
shelf. I did **not** confirm the tile-lattice-at-rotated-camera refinement —
that remains unproven and should not be asserted.

## Control

`land_near` (no water in frame) is `254a1daa20f715bf` in **every single arm** —
default, meander=0, mask=0, shoreline=0, and all three sweep amplitudes. The
gates touch only water, as intended.

## Proposal — NOT yet implemented, and one option is now disqualified

My earlier two candidates were justified on the grounds that they would be
"null-ops on mc2_05 and mc2_10 by construction, because those maps have no
mask". **That argument is void** — the mask does nothing anywhere on mc2_17, so
mask-conditioned fixes are not null-ops, they are no-ops. Withdrawn.

What the evidence actually supports:

1. **Do not lower the global default.** mc2_10 has no `shoreline_mask.png` and
   is driven by the same single meander term, so any global amplitude change
   moves the look the user approved at 75 on mc2_10. A global change cannot be
   proven safe here.
2. The defect is that amplitude is specified in **world units of thickness**
   while its visible reach is `A/|grad|` in **distance**. The right fix is the
   one already deferred for exactly this: **clamp by max horizontal distance
   rather than max thickness**, so a fixed amplitude cannot travel further than
   N wu inland regardless of how flat the shelf is. That bounds mc2_17 and
   mc2_01 without touching mc2_10's steeper shore, where the clamp would not
   bind.
3. Failing that, a **per-map amplitude** keyed off the mission sidecar.
